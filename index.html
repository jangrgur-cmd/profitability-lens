How the numbers are calculated

Revenue
volume × (pass-through + (list price − pass-through) × (1 − customer discount)) The discount applies only to our own fee. Third-party fees passed on at cost (Meta's WhatsApp fee, US carrier fees) are charged in full.
Delivery cost
volume × cost per message × exchange rate to EUR What we pay operators (SMS, voice), Meta (WhatsApp) or Viber. USD costs are converted at the EUR/USD rate.
Platform cost
volume × platform cost per message Our own infrastructure and support cost, so gross margin is not overstated.
Gross profit
revenue − delivery cost − platform cost
Gross margin
gross profit ÷ revenue Shown as "n/a" when revenue is zero.
Route
One country × channel combination, e.g. "Germany SMS". A route is loss-making when its gross profit over the selected period is below zero.
Whale curve
Customers sorted by gross profit, highest first, against their cumulative share of total gross profit. It rises above 100% when some customers lose money.
Revenue and cost per message
revenue ÷ messages and (delivery cost + platform cost) ÷ messages. Voice is counted per call.

    How the traffic data is made

    A small Node script (scripts/generate-data.mjs) turns the assumptions into one row per month, customer, country, channel and category. Monthly volume = the customer's estimated base volume × its share of traffic in that country × the channel's share × the category's share × a seasonality index × a little random noise. The noise comes from a seeded random number generator, so the same assumptions always produce exactly the same data. Revenue, cost and profit are not stored; they are calculated in the browser, so scenarios can recalculate them.
